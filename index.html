Info

Dimitris Ladopoulos - Art Director & Visual Designer. He discovered early-on his passion for game visuals, graphic design, motion-graphics and cinematography. He finds pleasure working across multiple disciplines and he never stops learning new skills and evolving his craft. He loves combining storytelling with visual design and large part of his work focuses on the relationship between technology, arts and ethics.

To tell the story, he mixes different techniques such as live action, stop-motion animation, CGI, motion design and photography. He always tries to push the limits, explore new techniques and overcome new challenges, combining storytelling with a strong sense of art direction.
