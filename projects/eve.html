EVE

Big things have small beginnings

The sequence captures the never-ending loop of death and birth. It begins with a closed-solid object. An external light pulse, is the trigger for the transformation. We see cables emerging, branches forming, escaping from the structure and growing into a massive tree-like structure. These branches reach-out and form a sphere that will eventually be the source of a new birth.
