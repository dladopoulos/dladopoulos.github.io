About

Hello I'm Dimitris, and I'm an Art Director & Visual Artist. I find pleasure working across multiple disciplines and I'm fascinated learning new skills and evolving my craft. I specialise in combining storytelling with visual design. As creative head of DGS, I have worked extensively across advertising, film and broadcast, among others.

Many of my films focus on the relationship between technology, arts and ethics. To tell the story, I mix many different techniques, including live action, stop motion animation, CGI, motion design and photography. I always try to push my limits, explore my style and travel beyond boundaries combining storytelling with a strong sense of art direction.

I love travelling and experiencing different cultures and viewpoints. I also enjoy working with different teams on inspiring briefs and exciting projects. So, don't hesitate to drop me a line!

Dimitris Ladopoulos
