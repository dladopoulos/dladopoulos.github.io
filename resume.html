Résumé

Hello! My name is Dimitris Ladopoulos. I am a an Art Director & Visual Designer. I specialise in combining storytelling with visual design. My work focuses on the convergence of technology, arts and ethics.

I was born and raised in Athens, Greece. I obtained my M.Eng on Product Design & Engineering from the Glasgow School of Arts and the University of Glasgow. I gained my experience in the UK at Honeywell and later at Inner Workings Gaming as a Web Designer and Art Director.

Back in Greece, I worked at Volax Interactive and X-Rated Productions as Head of Post Production. In 2000, I set up Deep Green Sea, heading a team of multi-disciplinary artists. Together, we created commissioned work for film and broadcast, as well as self-produced work such as the acclaimed series The Art of Making.

You can also view a showcase of my recent work, as well as an archive of previous projects.
